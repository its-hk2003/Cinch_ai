FAQ

Questions we always get asked.

Can I import data from Excel or Tally?+

Yes. During onboarding our team helps import client lists, employee records, vendor data and open jobs from Excel or CSV. The migration is handled for you.

Does it work on mobile phones?+

The technician portal works on any smartphone browser — Android or iPhone. No app download needed for Starter and Business. Enterprise includes native iOS and Android apps.

Is my business data safe? Where is it stored?+

Data is stored on AWS Mumbai servers — 100% in India. All connections are 256-bit SSL encrypted. Enterprise gets a dedicated database.

Will I be charged automatically after the trial?+

Never. No credit card is required to start. When your trial ends, your account pauses. Our team will reach out. You are never billed without your explicit confirmation.

Can I cancel anytime?+

Yes — cancel anytime with 30 days notice. No lock-in on monthly plans. Your complete data is exported to CSV before cancellation.
