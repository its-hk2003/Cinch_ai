02 —

No-Refund Policy

cinch AI maintains a strict no-refund policy on all subscription payments:

  • Monthly subscriptions: No refunds for the current or any previous billing month, regardless of usage
  • Annual subscriptions: No partial or full refunds for unused months within the annual billing period
  • Add-on features: No refunds once a feature has been activated
  • Early cancellation: If you cancel mid-period, your access continues until the end of the paid period. No refund is issued for the remaining days.
  • Non-use: Not using the platform during your subscription period does not entitle you to a refund

This policy is standard in the SaaS industry and is made possible by the availability of a free 7-day trial — giving you full opportunity to evaluate the platform before any payment.

03 —

Exceptions

cinch AI will consider a refund or credit only in the following circumstances:

  • Duplicate payment: You were charged more than once for the same billing period due to a technical error on our side. Full refund of the duplicate charge.
  • Service failure: The Platform was completely inaccessible for more than 72 consecutive hours due to a failure on cinch AI's side (not third-party infrastructure failure, force majeure, or your own network). In this case, a pro-rated credit for the downtime period may be offered.
  • Unauthorised charge: You can demonstrate that a charge was processed without your authorisation and you report it within 7 days of the charge date.
  • Platform discontinuation: cinch AI discontinues the platform entirely (see Section 8). A pro-rated refund of prepaid fees for the unused period will be issued.

To request an exception, email hemrajkmt2576606@gmail.com within 7 days of the charge with full supporting details. We will review and respond within 10 business days.

06 —

Failed Payments

  • If a payment fails, you will be notified by email immediately
  • You have a 7-day grace period to clear the outstanding amount; access continues normally during this period
  • If payment is not received within 7 days, your account will be suspended
  • If the account remains suspended for more than 30 days due to non-payment, it will be terminated and data deletion will begin
  • Reactivation after suspension is possible by clearing the outstanding balance; data will be intact during the suspension period
07 —

Trial Accounts

Trial accounts are completely free and require no payment. There is nothing to refund for a trial period.

  • At the end of the trial, if you do not convert to a paid subscription, your account becomes inactive
  • All trial data will be deleted 7 days after the trial ends, with a 3-day advance warning
  • Trial data does not carry over to a paid subscription as a credit or extension
08 —

Platform Discontinuation

If cinch AI decides to discontinue the platform entirely:

  • All active subscribers will receive at least 90 days' advance written notice
  • A pro-rated refund of any prepaid fees for the period after the discontinuation date will be issued
  • A minimum 60-day data export window will be provided
