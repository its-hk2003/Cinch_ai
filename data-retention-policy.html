Purpose

This Data Retention & Deletion Policy explains how long Cinch AI retains different categories of data, when and how data is deleted, and how you can request deletion or export. It applies to all Customers, trial users, and individual Data Principals whose data is processed through the platform.

This Policy complies with DPDPA 2023 S.8(7) (data must not be retained beyond the period necessary for the stated purpose) and the legal retention obligations under Indian law.

03 —

Financial Data Legal Hold — Important

⚠️ Your data deletion request cannot override Indian statutory law. Even after you cancel your subscription and your 30-day deletion window closes, Cinch AI is legally required to retain certain financial records under Indian law. This is called a "Legal Hold" and it overrides our standard 30-day deletion cycle for affected data only.

The following financial data will be retained in a secure, isolated legal archive even after subscription cancellation, for the periods required by law:

Record TypeMinimum RetentionGoverning Law
GST invoices, tax invoices, credit/debit notes5 years from end of financial yearCGST Act 2017, Section 36
Books of accounts, financial statements7 years from relevant assessment yearIncome Tax Act 1961, Section 44AA
Payroll records & employment registers (for companies)8 yearsCompanies Act 2013, Section 128
Payment gateway transaction records5 yearsPayment and Settlement Systems Act 2007; RBI guidelines

Data held under a Legal Hold is:

  • Stored in an isolated, encrypted archive with strictly controlled access
  • Not used for any business, analytical, or commercial purpose
  • Only accessible to Cinch AI when required to respond to a lawful government request, tax audit, or legal proceeding
  • Permanently deleted as soon as the statutory retention period expires

We will notify you in writing of any data held under a Legal Hold and the expected date of permanent deletion.

04 —

Deletion Process

When a deletion event is triggered:

  1. Day 0: Deletion event triggered (subscription cancellation confirmed, trial expiry, or explicit deletion request received)
  2. Day 0: Warning email sent to company admin explaining the 30-day window and how to request a data export
  3. Day 27: Reminder email sent with 3 days' notice before permanent deletion
  4. Day 30: All non-legally-held Customer Data is permanently deleted from the active database
  5. Within 30-day backup cycle: Deleted data is also purged from all backup copies as they rotate
  6. Within 5 business days of request: Deletion confirmation email sent upon request

⚠️ Deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once the 30-day window has passed, Cinch AI cannot recover any data under any circumstances. Please export your data before the deadline.

06 —

Right to Erasure

Under DPDPA 2023 S.12, individuals have the right to request erasure of their personal data. We handle such requests as follows:

  • Individual employee requests: Contact your company admin first. The admin can deactivate your account. Cinch AI will follow the standard retention schedule from the point of deactivation.
  • Company admin requests for full account deletion: Email us. We will process full account deletion within 10 business days, subject to legal hold obligations.
  • Limitations: We cannot erase data subject to a Legal Hold (Section 3). We will inform you of any such limitation in writing.

Submit erasure requests to: hemrajkmt2576606@gmail.com — Subject: "Data Erasure Request"

07 —

Backups

Cinch AI maintains automated database backups on a 30-day rolling cycle, hosted on AWS ap-south-1 (Mumbai, India) via Supabase.

  • All backups are encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS)
  • Access to backups is restricted to Cinch AI's founding team
  • Backups are used exclusively for disaster recovery — not for any analytical or commercial purpose
  • When data is deleted from the active database, it is automatically excluded from all subsequent backup cycles and purged from existing backups within the 30-day rotation
  • Legal Hold data in backups follows the extended retention periods in Section 3
