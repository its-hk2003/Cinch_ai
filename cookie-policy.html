05 —

Managing Cookies & Local Storage

Since all storage is strictly essential, disabling it will prevent the platform from functioning. If you clear cookies and local storage:

  • You will be logged out of the platform
  • Any offline job data not yet synced to the server may be lost
  • PWA preferences will be reset

You can manage cookies in your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

To clear PWA local data on Android: Settings → Apps → Cinch AI → Storage → Clear Data.
