Our Story

We built what service businesses actually needed.

Service businesses across India — repair shops, AMC providers, security companies, elevator services — were losing money every single month. Not because they were bad at their trade. Because they were running on WhatsApp and Excel.

Invoices going out 5 days late. AMC contracts expiring without a call. Payroll taking a weekend. Technicians getting the wrong jobs. Nobody able to answer the simple question: "How much did we make this month?"

"We built Cinch.AI because we watched this happen — and decided the solution had to be built specifically for how service businesses operate. Not adapted from something else."
