Security Violations

  • Attempting to access another company's data or admin account without authorisation
  • Attempting to bypass, disable, or circumvent any security feature
  • Reverse engineering, decompiling, or disassembling any part of the platform
  • Probing or scanning the vulnerability of the platform without prior written consent
  • Introducing malware, viruses, trojans, or any harmful code

Misuse of Access

  • Sharing login credentials between multiple individuals (each user must have their own account)
  • Allowing third parties outside your organisation to access your account without Cinch AI's written permission
  • Reselling, sublicensing, or white-labelling access to the platform
  • Using the platform to build a competing product or service

Technical Abuse

  • Using automated bots, scrapers, or scripts to extract data from the platform in bulk
  • Generating excessive load on the platform's servers (denial-of-service)
  • Manipulating API calls or platform requests in ways not intended by the product design

PWA / APK Specific

  • Distributing a modified version of the Cinch AI APK to any third party
